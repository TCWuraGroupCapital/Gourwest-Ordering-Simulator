Many Flavours. One Gourwest.

Born in Melbourne.
Shared with the World.

Founded in Melbourne in 2016, Gourwest started with an ambition: unite vibrant international street flavours with wholesome, fresh Australian produce.

From our early morning prep kitchens hand-cutting crisp Lebanese cucumbers and roasting sweet corn, to our bespoke open grills that seal in smoky marinades—we honour recipes that travel across borders while remaining grounded in everyday community dining.
