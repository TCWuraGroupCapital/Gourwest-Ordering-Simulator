## Brand & Style

### Personality & Purpose
The design system articulates an upscale, modern editorial fast-casual dining experience born out of Melbourne's culinary culture. Rooted in global gastronomy and regional craft, the interface balances culinary poise with seamless digital ordering speed. The atmosphere is grounded, hospitable, refined, and confident—conveying artisanal craft without excessive formality.

### Target Audience
Discerning urban diners, mobile-first takeaway guests, and corporate lunch patrons spanning Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, and Malaysia who value authentic flavours, dietary transparency, and frictionless digital ordering experiences.

### Design Movement
Modern Editorial Minimalist with Tactile Warmth:
- Deep botanical contrast through rich foliage tones paired against buttery, linen-toned surfaces.
- Editorial typographic pairings: expressive high-contrast serifs for titles and dish storytelling, anchored by geometric, human-centric sans serifs for transaction clarity.
- Soft container geometry with refined hair-line strokes and warm atmospheric elevations that replicate natural daylight in open-air dining spaces.
- Australian English localization across all system interfaces (`flavour`, `favourite`, `customise`, `speciality`).

## Colors

### Palette Philosophy
The palette pairs the natural depth of Victorian flora with warm culinary hospitality. The foundational deep forest green establishes quiet confidence and organic substance, while the warm cream base prevents the sterile chill of digital white canvases.

### Palette Implementation
- **Primary (`#173E32` - Forest Green):** Main navigation bars, primary CTA buttons, editorial headers, active category pills, and signature icon accents.
- **Secondary (`#B59A58` - Muted Gold):** Premium dish tags, member rewards accents, star ratings, borders on high-tier selections, and subtle metallic divider treatments.
- **Tertiary (`#C96D4B` - Restrained Terracotta):** Limited-time offers, hot spice badges, promotional calls-to-action, alerts, and cart notification counters.
- **Neutral Primary (`#242824` - Deep Charcoal):** High-contrast body copy, order summary numbers, price typography, and input labels.
- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base Canvas: Warm Cream (`#F7F3E8`) for the global page backdrop.
  - Surface Cards & Sheets: Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) to ensure food imagery and nutritional cards pop with clinical clarity.
  - Subtle Framing: Primary Green at 10% opacity (`rgba(23, 62, 50, 0.10)`) for card outlines and table rows.

## Typography

### Structural Pairings
- **Display & Section Titles:** *Playfair Display* lends editorial distinction, reminiscent of high-end culinary journals and tactile printed menus. Used selectively for hero claims, category milestones, and featured signature recipes.
- **Functional Body, Actions, & Numerics:** *Plus Jakarta Sans* handles navigation items, dietary matrices, item modifiers, prices, and status indicators with neutral, open-aperture legibility.

### Formatting Rules
- Display elements retain natural serif italic accents for provenance descriptors (e.g., *“Locally sourced from the Yarra Valley”*).
- Currency expressions pair body weights with semibold values without space between symbol and figure (e.g., `$24.50 AUD`).
- Microcopy badges and category chips utilize all-caps tracking with `label-sm` to maintain balance alongside heavy serif headers.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Architecture
The design system employs a flexible 12-column grid on desktop screens, collapsing gracefully into an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column column flow on mobile viewports:
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Max page container width of `1280px` centered with `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` column gutters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** `1.5rem` margins with `1.25rem` gutters for dual-column item discovery layouts.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** `1rem` outer margins with sticky bottom-anchored basket summaries and single-column full-width food cards.

### Spacing Principles
- Spacing follows strict multiples of `0.25rem` (4px baseline).
- Component interior spacing maintains generous room around food photography (`space-md` to `space-lg`) to reflect unhurried, comfortable dining.
- Section groupings use `space-xl` for breathing room between regional location finders, seasonal menus, and pickup time slots.

## Elevation & Depth

### Ambient Atmosphere
Surfaces lean heavily on soft warm light rather than synthetic, cold dropped shadows. Depth is achieved via multi-layered ambient spreads tinted with warm charcoal and muted umber:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#F7F3E8` Warm Cream canvas. Zero blur, flush presentation.
- **Level 1 (Menu Cards & Interactive Tiles):** `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(36, 40, 36, 0.05)`, encased in a delicate hair-line border: `1px solid rgba(23, 62, 50, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Active Modifiers, Hover Cards):** `0px 10px 28px -4px rgba(36, 40, 36, 0.08)`, border softened to `1px solid rgba(23, 62, 50, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets, Store Switcher, Order Drawer):** `0px 24px 48px -8px rgba(23, 62, 50, 0.16)`. Accompanied by a soft frosted glass backdrop `rgba(247, 243, 232, 0.85)` with `backdrop-blur(12px)`.

### Borders as Form
Outlines play an essential architectural role. Crisp 1px strokes tinted in Forest Green at low opacities define containers without cluttering the screen.

## Shapes

### Form Geometry
The shape language mirrors the clean lines of modern architectural dining spaces—organic yet disciplined:
- **Base Components (Input fields, standard cards, dropdowns):** `0.5rem` (`rounded-md` / `rounded`).
- **Signature Cards & Hero Modules:** `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) to `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`) giving a soft, tactile editorial feel to food imagery containers.
- **Interactive Badges, Dietary Filters, & Pill Buttons:** Fully rounded continuous pill geometry (`rounded-full`) to differentiate transactional utilities from structural editorial cards.
- **Image Treatment:** Dish pictures feature slight clipping radii (`0.75rem` to `1rem`) flush with parent card margins to frame culinary presentation like framed gallery plates.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Action ("Order Now", "Add to Bag"):** Solid Forest Green (`#173E32`) fill, Crisp White text (`#FFFFFF`), `0.5rem` border radius, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Smooth hover transition shifting to a slightly deeper forest tint with subtle lift.
- **Secondary Action ("Customise", "View Nutritional Info"):** Transparent background with a `1px` border in `#173E32`, text in `#173E32`. On hover, fills with `rgba(23, 62, 50, 0.05)`.
- **Tertiary Accent ("Redeem Reward"):** Muted Gold (`#B59A58`) fill with Crisp White text.

### Badges & Filter Chips
- **Category Chips:** Default state has a Warm Cream background with a `1px` border of `rgba(23, 62, 50, 0.12)`. Active state shifts to solid Forest Green fill with white text.
- **Dietary Badges (GF, VG, Halal):** `label-sm` uppercase, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `0.25rem 0.625rem`. Neutral tone `rgba(23, 62, 50, 0.08)` text `#173E32`.
- **Status & Promotional Badges ("Chef's Special", "New Season"):** Warm Terracotta (`#C96D4B`) solid background, white text, pill-shaped.

### Food & Product Cards
- **Base Layout:** Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) card structure resting on the Warm Cream backdrop. Includes a 16:10 or 1:1 food photography viewport, followed by dish title (`Playfair Display`), concise flavour notes (`Plus Jakarta Sans`), and bottom row anchoring the price, calorie count, and an "Add" icon button.
- **Border & Shadow:** `1px solid rgba(23, 62, 50, 0.08)` with Level 1 warm shadow.

### Form Fields & Selection Controls
- **Inputs:** Clean white field backgrounds with a `1px solid rgba(36, 40, 36, 0.15)` border, rounding at `0.5rem`. Focused state introduces a `#173E32` border with a 2px diffuse ring (`rgba(23, 62, 50, 0.10)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Custom circular radios and rounded checkboxes in `#173E32` when checked, with a crisp white checkmark or inner dot.

### Location & Pickup Metadata Strip
- Prominent header bar with pin indicator for current store selection (e.g., *“Melbourne Flinders Lane • Pickup ready in 15 mins”*). Features Muted Gold indicators for open hours and quick switcher trigger.
